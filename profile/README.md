# 🟠 Cozmetics

### Your Minecraft. Your style.

**Cozmetics** is a free Minecraft cosmetics ecosystem built to bring personal style into the game. Discover cosmetics, customize your look, and make your character feel like yours.

> ✦ One world. Endless ways to express yourself.

---

## ✨ What we're building

- 🎨 **Cosmetic Catalogue** · Browse cosmetics and preview your style.
- 🧍 **Player Preview** · See your character and customize your look.
- 🧩 **Fabric Mod** · Bring supported cosmetics into Minecraft Java Edition.
- 👕 **Wardrobe** · Organize your equipped cosmetics in one place.
- 🌐 **Connected Ecosystem** · A website, backend, and Discord integration designed to work together.

## 🪄 Our vision

Cozmetics aims to make cosmetic customization accessible, expressive, and easy to use. We're building toward a connected experience where your catalogue, wardrobe, and Minecraft client work together.

Our initial focus is Minecraft Java Edition and Fabric. Features and supported versions will be documented as development progresses.

## 🛠️ Built in the open

We're developing Cozmetics step by step, with a focus on clean design, useful features, and a lightweight experience. The project is still under active development, so features and compatibility may change.
